# Qualify the beta on measured Apple Silicon

Poptart will qualify the MVP beta on the exact physical Apple Silicon Mac recorded in its benchmark
report and release metadata. The first available baseline is the development M5 Pro. The 1.5-second
Completion Deadline, 99% pass rate, model residency, memory measurements, and artifact identity checks
remain unchanged.

Performance on an 8 GB M1 is unverified and must not be claimed or inferred from a slowdown factor,
artificial memory pressure, or another Apple Silicon generation. A later physical M1 measurement may
expand the supported hardware claim without blocking the beta. This supersedes the M1-specific release
requirements in ADRs 0039, 0041, 0043, 0051, and 0054; their runtime and fallback decisions remain active.
