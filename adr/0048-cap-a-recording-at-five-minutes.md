# Cap a Recording at five minutes

A single press-and-hold Recording will have a five-minute safety limit. The Indicator will begin a clear visual warning at four minutes and thirty seconds. At five minutes Poptart stops audio capture, finalizes recognition, and follows the normal Cleanup, deadline, insertion, or fallback path. This bound prevents a missed key-up event or stuck shortcut from leaving the microphone active indefinitely; the person may immediately begin another Dictation.
