# Speak outcomes in a toast, not in the Indicator

Poptart will give the Indicator one silhouette vocabulary and no color meaning. It remains persistent, compact, and nonactivating as ADR 0022 requires, sits at the bottom of the screen just above the Dock, and renders monochrome: a blank collapsed sliver when ready, a twenty-one bar waveform during Recording, and a spinner while finalizing, cleaning, and delivering. No outcome has a shape of its own, color encodes nothing, and the Indicator never contains words. Outcomes that need words are spoken by a separate Outcome Toast shown directly above the Indicator, carrying one short fixed message and dismissing itself. The Outcome Toast speaks only for outcomes that are not already evident on screen: “Copied to clipboard” for both the target-change result and the no-target Clipboard Dictation result, “Dictation failed” when no usable text exists, “Not available in a password field” for a secure control, and “Thirty seconds left” as a Recording approaches the five-minute limit. It stays silent whenever the delivered words are already at the insertion point, which covers cleaned insertion and the Raw Transcript, oversized-input, and Recognition Hypothesis Fallbacks, and stays silent for cancellation, which the person chose. Neither surface ever displays live or completed transcript text, and the Outcome Toast is limited to those fixed messages, so the full outcome and Fallback classification remains a Dictation Record fact rather than an on-screen one. This supersedes the Indicator appearance clauses of the earlier decisions: the copied-to-clipboard states of ADR 0046 and ADR 0052 become one shared message rather than two distinguishable states, the unavailable state of ADR 0047, the size-based Fallback display of ADR 0043, and the five-minute warning of ADR 0048 all move out of the Indicator and into the Outcome Toast or into history alone.
